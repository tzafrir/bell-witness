# Draft — data request to LMU Munich (Weinfurter group)

**To:** Prof. Harald Weinfurter group (LMU Munich / MPQ)
**Subject:** Per-event heralding records from your 2017 event-ready Bell test — pre-registered re-analysis

Dear Prof. Weinfurter and colleagues,

We are conducting a pre-registered re-analysis of archival loophole-free
Bell tests and are writing about your event-ready test with entangled atoms
(Rosenfeld et al., PRL 119:010402).

Our question needs exactly the records your Bell-state-measurement herald
produces. We would like to ask whether the following per-event data were
retained and could be shared:

1. BSM heralding records for each accepted event: which of the four APDs
   clicked, and the click times;
2. the corresponding setting bits and measurement outcomes at both atom
   stations (the ~tens of thousands of heralded events behind
   S = 2.221 ± 0.033, which we would reproduce first as a calibration gate
   before any new analysis).

The analysis is one sentence: a pre-registered test of whether the joint
statistics of one station's outcome together with the heralding-station
records depend on the remote station's setting — a quantity quantum
mechanics fixes at exactly zero, and which, as far as we can find, no
published analysis has examined before (existing no-signaling checks are
marginal; ours is the joint-with-witness version).

We commit before seeing any data: our pre-registration document (the exact
statistic, the allowed detector family, the permutation null, significance
thresholds, and the kill protocol applied to any apparent signal) is public
in our repository and we will send it up front. We have already run the
identical analysis on the Delft 2015/2016 open data, where the heralding
records are public (result: a clean null bounding the effect at
L_exp ≲ 0.105). Your dataset would roughly double the available
heralded-witness statistics and is the only other event-ready archive we
are aware of. The result is publishable either way and the data source is
credited in any output.

If the heralding records were not retained at per-event granularity, a
one-line reply saying so is itself a useful datum for our archival survey,
and we will not take more of your time.
