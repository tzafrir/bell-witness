A Bell Test with a Monitored Witness:
probing extended no-signaling in the joint statistics of system and environment

Proposal v0 · companion to translation-protocol.md, t4_impossibility.md, literature-survey.md, and lemma2-lab-notes.html (v4)

proposal v0observable: L_expQM predicts exactly 0

The claim in one breath. Quantum mechanics makes an exact, untested prediction: when a partially decohered entangled pair is measured, the joint statistics of one wing's outcome together with any record held by the decohering environment are perfectly independent of the remote wing's measurement setting — zero dependence, at every decoherence level, forever. A broad class of classical substrate models — any model in which outcomes are computed from underlying configurations — provably cannot achieve this: in such models, Bell violation forces the remote setting to be written into local micro-statistics, at magnitudes we have measured to be large. One experiment separates them, and nobody has run it.

1 · The observable

Definition Source emits entangled pairs to wings A (Alice, settings a) and B (Bob, settings b). An ancilla C interacts with the pair near the source with tunable strength φ (a controlled, monitored decoherence channel), and is measured in basis c, spacelike separated from Bob's setting choice. Define

Lexp(φ) = maxa,c | E[A·C | a, c, b=0] − E[A·C | a, c, b=1] |

Quantum prediction: Lexp ≡ 0 exactly. Bob's operation acts on his tensor factor and commutes with every observable of A⊗C; this is no-signaling extended to the joint system-plus-environment level — a theorem of the formalism, not an approximation, and independent of φ.
Intuition A crime in two cities, one suspect interviewed in each. Standard Bell tests check that neither suspect's own answers betray which question the other was asked — and they don't. But there was a witness at the scene: the environment that brushed against the pair at birth. Quantum mechanics swears the witness is perfectly discreet — not even the pattern of agreement between the witness and a suspect can betray the remote question. Classical substrate models cannot keep the witness quiet, because their spooky correlations work by physically jostling a shared hidden object, and the witness was touching the object when the jostling happened.

2 · Why this is unmeasured

Every located experimental no-signaling test checks marginal conditions — that one party's outcome distribution is independent of the other's setting (dedicated analyses of loophole-free data; two-proportion Z-tests as standard practice). Speed-of-influence experiments (Salart 2008; Yin 2013) bound finite-speed propagating mechanisms via frame scanning. Leggett-model falsifications (Gröblacher 2007; Branciard 2007/08) exclude nonlocal models with specific marginal structure. Quantum Darwinism experiments demonstrate fragment-resolved environment readout — the needed lab capability — but never in a Bell configuration conditioned on a remote setting. Full map with references: literature-survey.md.

Intuition Everyone has checked whether each suspect's mood changes with the other's interrogation. Nobody has ever sat the witness and a suspect down together and asked whether their pattern of agreement shifts. The substrate class passes every existing test by construction — its marginals are pinned flat by symmetry. The joint with the witness is its only exposed skin. The theorem (§3) says that skin cannot be armored.

3 · The substrate-side prediction

Theorem (T4 impossibility; proof in t4_impossibility.md) In any model with setting-independent preparation where outcomes are functions of side-local configurations, Bell violation implies an A-side observable whose joint statistics with A depend on b. Closest prior art: Bancal et al., Nat. Phys. 8:867 (2012) — finite-speed hidden influences imply superluminal signaling. Same logical shape (hidden mechanism ⇒ operational signaling); different axis (propagation speed there; configuration-disturbance with accessible records here), and here with measured magnitudes.
Measured magnitudes (contractive rewrite family; lemma2-lab-notes.html v4) Optimal-detector leak ≈ 0.25–0.30 at S = 2.07–2.48 (15–26× statistical floor); not suppressible by adversarial optimization (0/7 gadgets; dedicated minimizer failed at 26σ). Mechanism law: leak ∝ violating-mechanism rate, not S−2 — confirmed on two path families; leak per unit violation grows toward the classical boundary, i.e. the effect is relatively strongest exactly where real partially-decohered pairs live. Robustness: record-fidelity cost sub-linear (58% of leak survives at 60% fidelity); coverage cost ≈ proportional. Realistic partial, noisy records: expected Lexp ~ 0.05–0.15 for this family.
Intuition Three findings, one message. The gossip is loud (a 0.3 shift where zero is predicted). The gossip survives bad hearing (blurry records keep most of the signal — many small records vote, and majorities are hard to silence). And diluted entanglement gossips more per unit of spookiness, not less — because the leak tracks how often the mechanism fires, not how much advantage survives. You cannot whisper this mechanism. That is not a tuning accident; it is what the theorem looks like when it has numbers.

4 · Apparatus

  1. Source: polarization-entangled photon pairs, standard loophole-free-grade geometry, fast random setting generators for a, b.
  2. Monitored decoherence stage: ancilla photon (or atomic ancilla) weakly coupled to one photon near the source, interaction strength φ tunable from 0 (S ≈ 2.8) toward severance (S → 2). This is the substrate model's "partial severance with records" regime by construction: the ancilla demonstrably causes the decoherence it records.
  3. Third detector: measures the ancilla in a scanned set of bases c, spacelike from Bob's choice. Quantum-Darwinism experiments establish fragment-resolved readout feasibility.
  4. Output: for each (a, b, c): the full joint table P(A, C | a, b, c), alongside S(φ).
Intuition It is an ordinary Bell experiment with one deliberate act of eavesdropping: we let a single photon watch the pair's birth closely enough to blur it a little, then we never stop interviewing that photon. The blurring is the point — the substrate's leak lives at partial decoherence, and we control the dial.

5 · Protocol and statistics

Plan (i) Pre-registration of φ grid, c-basis scan, test statistic, and corrections — mirroring this program's internal discipline. (ii) Test statistic: max over (a, c) of the b-shift in E[A·C], with a train/test split (bases selected on half the data, effect sized on the holdout) to kill selection inflation — the same protocol our simulations used. (iii) Statistics: bounding Lexp < δ needs ~δ−2 trials per setting cell; δ = 10−2 ⇒ ~104 trials/cell — hours, not months, for modern sources. (iv) Analysis frameworks for data with bounded signaling exist (arXiv:2602.05507; arXiv:2511.06624) and should be adopted for the null analysis. (v) Report at several φ: the substrate effect peaks at intermediate decoherence and must vanish at φ=0 and φ=1 (no records / no violation) — a built-in signature shape that separates it from mundane systematics, which have no reason to track φ this way.
Intuition The cheapest profound experiment available: the statistics are easy because the predicted effect is either exactly zero or enormous by precision-measurement standards. And it carries its own lie detector — the effect must rise and fall with the blur dial in a specific arch, while boring systematic errors don't know what the dial is.

Escape hatches, audited in advance A null does not kill all substrate models — it forces a structural cost. The retreat "the lab ancilla doesn't hold the substrate's records" contradicts the record⇒sever unification (the ancilla demonstrably causes the decoherence; in the model, causing decoherence is taking records), reopening the axiom triad and forcing micro-inaccessibility as a brute, unexplained axiom against the grain of the T4 theorem. The retreat "wrong detector basis" is closed quantitatively: the in-model leak is broadband across single-coordinate detectors; a moderate basis scan covers it. We commit to these audits now, before data exist.
Intuition Heads: the first confirmation of a quantum prediction nobody bothered to test, plus a fence around an entire class of "the universe is secretly classical" theories, with coordinates. Tails: the discovery of the century, to be disbelieved until it survives everyone's best attempts to kill it — including ours. The arch over the blur dial is the referee.

7 · Retrospective appendix — a free first bound

The 2015–2016 loophole-free experiments (Delft, NIST, Vienna) partially published raw data. Any auxiliary detector channel temporally correlated with the source — heralding detectors, monitor photodiodes, environment channels recorded for diagnostics — can serve as a crude C. We invite (and intend to attempt) a retrospective analysis: tabulate b-conditioned joints of (A, auxiliary channel). Sensitivity will be poor (the channels were not designed as record-bearing ancillas; coverage and fidelity unknown), but the bound costs nothing, and defining the observable publicly is itself the mitigation against the survey's residual risk of an unindexed prior measurement.

8 · Scope and claims discipline

  • This proposal does not test "the universe is a graph." It tests extended no-signaling at the joint system-environment level — a sharp, theory-agnostic question on which QM stakes an exact 0 and a provably-leaky model class stakes a large number.
  • Magnitudes (0.05–0.3) are properties of the studied family, not of all conceivable substrates; the theorem guarantees existence of a leak for the whole class, the family supplies the scaling laws that convert a null into exclusion surfaces.
  • All registered predictions, broken predictions, discarded runs, and the two flukes caught by confirmation protocols are in the master record (lemma2-lab-notes.html v4). The methodology of this program is offered as part of the proposal: pre-registration is not decoration; it is how this document earned its numbers.
